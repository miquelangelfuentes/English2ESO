01

CONTRAST

Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple

Hàbits, rutines i fets habituals

subject + verb (+ s/es)

I usually eat at home.

The policeman follows the criminal.

alwaysusuallyevery dayoften

Present Continuous

Accions que passen ara o situacions temporals

subject + am/is/are + -ing

Today I'm eating at a restaurant.

In the picture, he is playing.

nowat the momenttodayLook!
💡 Truc per decidir

Pregunta't: «És una rutina o passa normalment?» → Simple. «Està passant ara o és temporal?» → Continuous.

02

IMPORTANT EXCEPTION

Stative verbs · verbs estàtics

Alguns verbs expressen estats, no accions: gustos, sentiments, pensaments, possessió o percepció. Normalment no els posem en Present Continuous.

♥ Gustoslike · love · hate · prefer
☁ Mentknow · believe · understand · remember
◉ Sentitssee · hear · smell · taste
◆ Possessióhave · own · belong
Watch out!

I'm knowing the answer. → I know the answer.

Alguns canvien de significat: I think your drawing is nice (opinió), però I'm thinking about travelling (reflexionant).

03

CONFIRMATION

Tag questions

Una tag question és una pregunta curta al final d'una frase. Serveix per confirmar informació o buscar l'acord de l'altra persona.

Frase afirmativa+tag negativa

She likes this book, doesn't she?

Frase negativa+tag afirmativa

They aren't sleeping, are they?

FraseTag
He is happy,isn't he?
You have got a car,haven't you?
She likes this book,doesn't she?
We will be late,won't we?
🧩 Com la construeixo?
  1. Busca l'auxiliar: is, have, does, will…
  2. Canvia'l de positiu a negatiu (o a l'inrevés).
  3. Repeteix el subjecte amb un pronom: Anna → she; the boys → they.

03 · SUPPORT

Encara tens dubtes?

1

Busca la pista temporal

Every day, usually → Simple.
Now, at the moment → Continuous.

2

Mira l'auxiliar

A les tag questions, reutilitza is, have, will… Si no n'hi ha, fes servir do/does.

3

Equivocar-se ajuda

Llegeix l'explicació després de cada resposta i torna-ho a provar. No hi ha límit d'intents.
